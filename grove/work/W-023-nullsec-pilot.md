## Outcome

Use the new Grove for one real nullsec change through interactive shaping,
implementation, review and local integration, and decide whether to continue
using it. This supplies W-018's external adoption evidence.

## Scope and bounds

Read nullsec's current instructions and retrieve predecessor knowledge through
its `grove status`, `grove find` and `grove context`. Rehearse migration in a
disposable copy first. Preserve IDs or an explicit collision mapping, links,
vocabulary, decisions, open questions, current work and valuable historical
evidence. Do not make every old type a new required schema type.

Use W-030's general knowledge support for content that does not need an
operational contract. D-006 separates ordinary identity/placement stability
from an explicit migration: W-029 reconciles this repo's entire Grove corpus,
including IDs and locations. Use its lessons to propose a coherent nullsec
target and map every retained identity/location that changes. Do not introduce
ongoing completion-driven filing. Historical folders and unsupported content
must be accounted for in the rehearsal rather than carried forward by default.
W-029 supplies a rehearsal, not a blanket mapping or live-write authority for
nullsec's different content; the owner selects that cutover separately.

After rehearsal, present the exact live migration, binary/workflow version,
authority cutover, chosen work and rollback procedure for the owner's assignment.
This proposed record does not authorize sibling writes. At cutover each fact
has one editable owner; keep archived material clearly historical. Stop using
predecessor skills for the migrated scope. Keyborg is the second adoption test,
shaped from this evidence rather than migrated alongside nullsec automatically.

## Acceptance

1. A rehearsal inventory maps retained knowledge and relationships, identifies
   unsupported or deliberately archived content, validates links and records,
   and demonstrates rollback without losing source material.
2. The owner selects and authorizes the exact live migration and real change.
3. Fresh interactive sessions complete shape → implement → review → integrate
   using Grove-owned entrypoints with attributable evidence and human judgment.
4. The owner can identify what changed, what needs attention and the next action
   from durable records; predecessor skills are unnecessary for that loop.
5. Record adoption friction, unresolved limits and the owner's continue/revise
   verdict; update W-018 acceptance and shape only justified follow-up work.

## Preparation and next

No implementation plan exists yet. Create a linked plan through the supported
CLI after W-022. Perform read-only inventory and disposable rehearsal before asking
for live cutover approval. Do not select a hobby feature from stale conversation
context; investigate current project priorities with the owner.
