## Meaning

The activity that makes [work](G-054-work.md) ready to implement: inspecting
the real state, investigating technical unknowns, surfacing the choices only a
person can make, and writing a plan when the change is more than small and
obvious. It happens inside an assignment's mandate and does not widen it.

Preparation is an activity, not a status and not an approval gate: a technical
plan does not wait for a human sign-off unless it needs a product choice the
work does not make. A caller may bound an attempt at its plan
([G-134](G-134-bound-an-attempt-at-its-plan-and.md)); that is the end of the
caller's mandate, not a gate the plan needs.

## Relationships

Precedes implementation within an [attempt](G-056-attempt.md). Its durable
output is a plan, or a "no plan needed" note in the work's Next, and sometimes
a question.
