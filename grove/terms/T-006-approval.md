## Meaning

A person with authority, or someone they delegated to, accepting one specific
[candidate](T-004-candidate.md) as meeting the work's outcome. It is bound to
that candidate: if the candidate changes, the approval does not carry over and
must be reconsidered.

Not a [review](T-005-review.md), which informs it; not passing checks, which
cannot supply judgment; and not [integration](T-007-integration.md), which
follows it. Grove does not record approvals yet; today the owner's verdict is
quoted in the work record.

## Relationships

Follows review, precedes integration.
