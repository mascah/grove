  no code.

  is settled only when the owner answers it, and your inference stays open,
  said as such.

  change what is proposed, name it and ask whether to prototype it; build
  nothing yourself. If the owner agrees, run Grove's `instructions
  prototype "<question>"` and follow what it prints, here.

  listed below; it is used only once the owner confirms.
- When the owner asks for a proposal, run Grove's `instructions propose`
  with no argument, adding `--schema <name>` if the owner confirmed
  `bugfix` or named another listed schema, and follow what it prints, here.
