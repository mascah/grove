`proposal.md` states the problem, the intended outcome, the scope, the
consequential decisions and the open questions.

- Title: write it in `.grove.yaml` and repeat it as the proposal's first
  heading.
- Problem: cite observations by their source instead of restating them as
  fact.
- Decisions: only choices the owner made, in this conversation and in the
  round before writing, that constrain the work or would be costly to
  reverse. Quote the owner and give the reason.
- What you infer or propose is not a decision, however likely. Ask it in the
  round; what the owner leaves open goes under open questions. A proposal
  lists no assumptions.
- Open questions: say which must be answered before review and which can
  wait. Name the task a question blocks when it decides how that task is
  built: `/grove-implement --unattended` leaves that task blocked until the
  proposal answers it, while `/grove-implement` asks the owner.
- Related documents: link each change spec and the tasks.
